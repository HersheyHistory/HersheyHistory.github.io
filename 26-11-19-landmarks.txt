HERSHEY HISTORY HUNT - SITE INFORMATION
=======================================

HOW TO USE THIS FILE

  * Each site is a block between the ########... lines. One block per site.
  * Every line is a LABEL, a colon, and the value. Type your answer after
    the colon. Where it is blank, we don't have that information yet.
  * HISTORY and FULL HISTORY can run to several paragraphs. Put a blank
    line between paragraphs. They continue until the next LABEL.
  * Please leave the LABELS themselves alone. Change the LATITUDE,
    LONGITUDE and photo file lines only if you mean to.
  * Save as plain text (.txt) when you are done.

WHAT EACH LABEL MEANS

  TITLE               Full name, shown on the answer page.
  SHORT TITLE         Short name for the final score screen.
  PHOTO YEAR          Four-digit year the OLD photo was taken. Players are
                      scored against this. Leave blank if unknown - they
                      then get full marks for the year.
  NOW PHOTO YEAR      Year the present-day photo was taken.
  HINT                One line shown to players BEFORE they guess.
                      Do not give away the name of the place.
  HISTORIC LABEL      Caption under the old photo on the answer page.
  MODERN LABEL        Caption under the present-day photo.
  LATITUDE/LONGITUDE  Where the site is. A site with these blank stays out
                      of the game until they are filled in.
  THEN PHOTO          File name of the old photo.
  NOW PHOTO           File name of the present-day photo.
  ARCHIVE LINK        Text and web address of the PastPerfect search shown
                      under "More historical images". Add another pair of
                      lines for a second link.
  HISTORY             The story shown on the answer page.
  FULL HISTORY        Longer version behind the "Read full story" button.
                      Optional.

HISTORY:
This building still remains, is still beautiful, and is used by many, especially during the summer months. It was built during the depression and was designed after a building Milton Hershey saw in the Mediterranean. If you have been to Hershey and looked around you can easily see this building. It sits on “Pat’s Hill”, although the history behind the name of the hill is a mystery.  From this building you can see Hersheypark, the Milton S Hershey Medical Center, the Hershey factory smokestacks, and almost all of Hershey.



FULL HISTORY:

It originally had 170 rooms
It was completed during the depression, one of many projects M S Hershey did to both build things cheaply and keep people employed. Other 1930s projects included the Hershey Arena (now Hersheypark Arena) and the Hershey Community Center, which still remains but is not open to the public.
It was based on a 30 room hotel Mr Hershey saw in the Mediterranean that overlooked the ocean.
During the depression it was reportedly so empty that someone rode a horse across the lobby and down the hallways. 
The Circular Dining Room was built so nobody would have to sit in a corner (nobody puts Baby in a corner!). Nobody had a bad seat. 
The Fountain Lobby was based on Mr Hershey’s love of Cuba, where he had a large sugar plantation.
It is currently an AAA Four Diamond rated resort.

HISTORY:
Built in 1806, long before Hershey ever existed, this house still stands at a major intersection. It is built of brownstone, a common building material around here because there were large brownstone quarries nearby. The house still looks very much like this, but the porch is different. It is still above the level of the road, but a low stone wall now surrounds it. The town it was in was named after the first resident of this house, and some still call the area by that name.
